How do guests open the invitation?+

They simply tap the link you send on WhatsApp. It opens immediately in their phone browser — no app download or login needed.

Do I have to type the Urdu text myself?+

No! You only fill the order form in English. Our team and calligraphic engine prepare all Urdu Nastaliq names, parents' titles, and event headings for you.

Can I update timings or venue after sharing?+

Yes. Let us know anytime and every link you already shared on WhatsApp updates instantly.
