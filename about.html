06 / Questions

Asked often, answered here.

Have a custom request or want to add a Qawwali Night, Mayun, or Dholki? You can add unlimited custom ceremonies directly in the order form.

How do separate links for Mehndi, Baraat, and Walima work? +

When we build your invitation, we generate dedicated links for every combination of your events — for example /all for close family, /barat-walima for relatives, and /walima for colleagues. Each link automatically hides the other events so guests only see the functions they are invited to.

Do I need to type the Urdu text myself? +

No! You only need to fill the order form in English. Our team and calligraphic engine prepare all Urdu Nastaliq names, parents' titles, dates, and ceremony headings for your review.

Can I change the dates, timings, or venue after placing my order? +

Yes, anytime. Unlike printed cards, digital invitations can be updated in minutes if a venue or timing changes — and the same link you already shared on WhatsApp will immediately show the updated details.

Do guests need to download an app to open the invitation? +

Never. Your invitation opens directly inside any smartphone browser when tapped from WhatsApp, iMessage, Instagram, or email, with smooth animations and clear Urdu/English legibility for elders.
